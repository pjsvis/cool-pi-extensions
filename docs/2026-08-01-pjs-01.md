

- LLM substrates are evaluative rather than deterministic

- we can aim for deterministic adequacy for a given task

- edge-lord substrates are deterministically adequate for most tasks but they
  cost a lot

- muppet-substrates fail in a deterministically manner regardless of cost

- daily-driver-substrates should be deterministically adequate and able to carry
  out tasks in an adequately deterministic manner at an acceptable cost

- to make sure of the above we use an epistemic-constraint-stack

- and a deterministic context-initialisation phase

- after that its up to the agent to maintain its state and eventually hand off
  to the next agent-session

- agent-sessions are kept short and typically cover a phase of an epic

- an epic is typically generated from a brief which has a playbook that
  determines its structure

- playbooks describe the structure of briefs, debriefs, decisions, and playbooks
  themselves

- things like sqlite have a playbook for the specific way they are used in this
  context/project/repo

- the repo is where all of the artefacts of the project are persisted and
  revisioned

- agents can orient themselves to the project through a deteministic justfile
  recipe

- agents can also pick up the current persisted context and use it to continue a
  phase of an epic

- epics are divided into phases which are implemented sequentially by agents
  with fresh context windows

- the persisted artefacts are the context and the agents use them to determine
  what to do next
